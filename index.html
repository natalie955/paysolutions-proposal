Dependencies

What Moves the Timeline

Three things decide whether implementation lands nearer four weeks or six. All three are manageable — but they're worth knowing up front.

Access to the right people

Discovery only works if we can sit with whoever actually runs each process — not just whoever is free. A couple of focused hours from the right person beats a full day with the wrong one.

Blueprint turnaround

We don't build until the design is signed off. If the blueprint sits waiting for a decision, the whole timeline shifts with it — so we agree a review date when we hand it over.

Scheduling the onsite days

Two consecutive days with your team in one room takes planning around your month-end and payroll cycles. We book these dates early and work backwards from them.
